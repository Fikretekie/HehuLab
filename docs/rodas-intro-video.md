## Steps
1. Fik checks the Tigrinya, then adds the 15 lines to Adiam's folder on the voices site.
2. Adiam records them (one take each, smiling while she speaks: it can be heard).
3. Run her recordings through the voice changer (safety rule, TASKS.md #26).
4. Kling: make the extra faces (waving, ear, fingers, laughing) from the same base picture, then use
   **Lip Sync** with her audio so the mouth matches the words.
5. Claude joins the pieces into one 1-minute video of only Rodas, with Tigrinya + English captions and the
   HehuLab logo.
6. Put it in "Meet Rodas" on the landing page (with a play button) and later on YouTube.
